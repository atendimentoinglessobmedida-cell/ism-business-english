# Premium content expansion

P9 adds seven bilingual lessons: price negotiation, suppliers in China, Indian English at work, British English at work, corporate acronyms/abbreviations/anagrams, blockchain, and legal/tax English.

The seven lessons add 42 bilingual phrase pairs, 74 vocabulary entries, 21 objective exercises, seven writing tasks and seven oral practice tasks. Each includes a fictional dialogue, transcript, comprehension question, feedback, model response and self-review criteria. Sources are linked within relevant lessons. Legal/tax material teaches language with jurisdiction context; it does not prescribe rates or individual advice. Regional English lessons avoid treating a country as one accent.

P3–P8 also receive 50 original dialogues and 50 comprehension exercises. These are optional: existing completion requirements and saved completions remain valid. P9 comprehension is required before first completion. Core, P1 and P2 lesson content is unchanged. The catalogue now contains 95 Premium lessons overall.

Audio uses the existing device speech synthesis player, with normal/slow playback and transcripts. This release does not provide recordings of authentic regional speakers or automatic pronunciation assessment. Voice availability depends on the device. Physical Android playback has not been verified in this release.

Validation: run `.ci/premium-check.cjs`, `.ci/premium-special-check.cjs` and `.ci/premium-dialogues-check.cjs` with Node. They check course schema, routes, answer keys, price calculations, separate storage keys, required P9 comprehension and optional legacy practice.
