## Pedagogical choices
Use idioms alongside plain-English paraphrases, distinguish actual results from targets and forecasts, and avoid equating revenue, profit and cash timing. Supplier practice distinguishes quotations, invoices, internal approval and conditional delivery estimates. Exercises use fictional figures and documents. Cultural stereotypes, unsupported salary statistics and older legal equivalences from the video description were excluded. Dialogues, explanations and examples are newly written; no source article, image or video is republished.
