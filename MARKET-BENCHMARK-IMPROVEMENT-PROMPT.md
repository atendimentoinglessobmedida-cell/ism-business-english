# ISM Business English — Market Benchmark Improvement Prompt

Date: 2026-09-27

## Objective
Apply proven language-learning product principles to ISM without turning it into a generic language app. Preserve Business English positioning, existing progress, persistence, PWA behavior and all working learning flows.

## Evidence distilled from current market benchmark

- Babbel: short practical lessons, real-life dialogues, speech recognition with immediate pronunciation feedback and slower playback; AI speaking scenarios provide hints in a low-pressure environment.
- ELSA: scenario-based AI role-play followed by detailed speaking feedback.
- Speak: speaking-first design, custom conversational scenarios, adaptive tutor and learner-generated personalized speaking lessons.
- Pimsleur: listen/recall cycles, Speak Easy role-play transcripts, saved vocabulary, Voice Coach feedback, offline learning and daily challenges.
- Rosetta Stone: contextual immersion, progressive movement from meaning to conversation, pronunciation tooling and conversation missions.
- Memrise: authentic native-speaker video/audio and exposure to real accents rather than only synthetic/textbook speech.

## Product principles to transfer

1. Speaking must be a repeated performance loop: Prepare → Speak → Listen back → Diagnose → Retry → Save evidence.
2. Practice should increasingly move from controlled prompts to contextual professional production.
3. Audio should expose learners to realistic pace, accent variation and connected speech, with optional slower replay rather than permanently simplified input.
4. The next recommended activity must be explainable from learner evidence.
5. Review must retrieve language from prior errors, weak skills and elapsed time rather than random repetition.
6. Learners should be able to create/revisit professional practice around an immediate real-world need.
7. Progress should emphasize professional mastery and evidence, not infantilizing rewards.
8. Every market-inspired feature must be adapted to meetings, presentations and professional communication.

## Execution packages

### MB1 — Speaking Intelligence
Enhance the existing Speaking Experience rather than replacing it. Add a structured diagnostic model covering clarity, fluency, pronunciation/intelligibility, professional register, useful vocabulary and action orientation where evidence is available. Never fabricate acoustic scores when the browser cannot measure them. Always end with one prioritized “Next improvement” and offer Retry. Retain compact attempt evidence in My Business English.

### MB2 — Professional Scenario Studio
Extend Real Business English with reusable scenario templates for meetings, presentations, negotiation, client calls, disagreement, clarification, deadlines, problem solving and Q&A. Allow a learner to select an immediate professional need and generate a guided practice path from existing content/templates. Prefer deterministic local templates unless an authenticated AI backend exists; do not expose secrets client-side.

### MB3 — Authentic Listening Ladder
For suitable activities use Listen → Understand → Notice → React. Support normal and slower replay. Tag or organize accent exposure where authentic recordings exist. Do not claim accent authenticity for synthetic TTS. Preserve transcripts as optional scaffolding rather than the default first exposure when pedagogically appropriate.

### MB4 — Adaptive Coach Evidence
Make Coach recommendations explicitly cite learner evidence: due review, recent error, weak lesson/skill, speaking evidence, goal and recency. Show a concise “Why this now?” explanation. Add confidence/fallback logic so sparse histories produce honest general recommendations.

### MB5 — Smart Review Intelligence
Strengthen existing Smart Review 2.0 with recency, prior errors, weak skills, retrieval history and interleaving. Prevent repeated easy items from crowding out weak material. Keep review sessions short and explain why an item returned when useful.

### MB6 — My Business English Portfolio
Turn the existing hub into a professional evidence portfolio: recurring errors, saved language/chunks, strongest productions, recent speaking attempts, review improvements and scenario history. Prefer useful evidence over vanity metrics.

### MB7 — Business Mode “Need it now”
Create rapid professional preparation flows such as “Meeting in 15 minutes”, “Present results”, “Disagree politely”, “Handle a difficult question” and “Negotiate a deadline”. Each flow should provide Essential language → Listen → Mini practice → Speak → Quick simulation, reusing existing content where possible.

### MB8 — Adult Motivation & Mastery
Retain XP/stars only where already useful. Add professional mastery milestones tied to demonstrated actions (e.g. handling clarification, presenting a recommendation) rather than arbitrary tapping/streak pressure. Do not add manipulative or childish mechanics.

### MB9 — Benchmark Regression & Final Audit
Add automated contracts for the new integrations, ensure no double XP, preserve Core/Premium storage separation, offline behavior, accessibility, mobile layout and reduced-motion behavior. Run existing QA plus new contracts. Keep release candidate Draft and unpublished until all automated gates are green and physical-device requirements are documented.

## Rejected market patterns

- No leaderboard or social pressure merely because competitors use engagement mechanics.
- No fabricated AI/pronunciation scoring without a real measurement engine.
- No always-online dependency for core lessons.
- No generic open chat that weakens the Business English positioning.
- No autoplay microphone/audio behavior.
- No replacement of proven structured pedagogy with unconstrained AI conversation.
- No copying proprietary copy, visual design or content.

## Definition of done

For every package: inspect existing implementation → make the smallest high-impact adaptation → add/extend regression contract → run QA → fix failures before advancing. Preserve accessibility, local-first recovery, offline shell and learner state. The final report must distinguish implemented capabilities from features that require an authenticated AI/speech backend, licensed human recordings, or physical-device validation.
